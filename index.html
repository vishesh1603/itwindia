ITW was founded in 1912 and is a global manufacturer working across many industries. Its seven segments all run on one proprietary business model, which lets them grow steadily with best-in-class margins and returns.

The company is committed to its enterprise strategy: delivering stronger performance over the long term and standing among the best performing, highest-quality and most respected industrial companies. ITW India Private Limited brings this capability to customers in India from its office in Gurgaon.
