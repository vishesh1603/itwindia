Business Segments

A diversified, high-quality business portfolio.

ITW’s portfolio is made up of seven market-leading segments, each in an industry where product performance matters most to the customer and the best solution wins. The businesses operate around the world so they can stay close to their customers.

In India, ITW brings together deep local engineering capabilities and global brand leadership across automotive assembly, infrastructure fastening, commercial food equipment, specialized polymer adhesives, niche packaging, precision test systems, and advanced arc welding solutions.
