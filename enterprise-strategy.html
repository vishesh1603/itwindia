Enterprise Strategy

Delivering solid growth with best-in-class margins and returns.

Through its Enterprise Strategy, ITW has shown the strength and competitive edge of the ITW Business Model in industry.

The strategy began in 2012 with the aim of delivering solid growth with best-in-class margins and returns. Between 2012 and 2023 operating margin rose by more than nine percentage points, earnings per share and market capitalisation more than tripled, dividends per share grew 3.7 times, and total shareholder return beat both the S&P 500 and ITW’s peer group.

The Next Phase

The Next Phase, which started in 2024, makes organic growth the top priority. By 2030 ITW intends to make organic growth a core strength, on a par with its financial performance and operational skill.

Customer-Back Innovation is the biggest driver of that growth. ITW will build this capability with the same rigour it used to improve 80/20 Front-to-Back over the past decade.

Progress also depends on attracting, developing and keeping great ITW leaders and talent. ITW aims to be a great employer, and the ITW Business Model gives people the tools to focus on what matters most.

2012 – 2017

Portfolio management & 80/20 baseline implementation across global divisions.

2018 – 2023

Operating margin expansion to 26.3% and disciplined capital allocation.

2024 – 2030

Organic growth leadership powered by customer-back innovation and talent.
