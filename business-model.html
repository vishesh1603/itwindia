ITW Business Model

The ITW Business Model is a powerful source of competitive advantage.

The ITW Business Model is a proprietary mix of strategic, operational and cultural practices that has been developed and refined inside the company for more than 30 years. It is ITW’s defining competitive advantage, and it is the value ITW adds to every one of its divisions.

It has three elements: the 80/20 Front-to-Back Process, Customer-Back Innovation, and a Decentralized, Entrepreneurial Culture.

A Model for Success

80/20 Front-to-Back Process: how we operate

This trade-secret set of tools and methods helps each division structure its business around its largest and most profitable customers, and cut the cost, complexity and distraction of serving very small ones. The result is first-rate execution in front of the customer, high-quality organic growth, and superior profitability and returns on capital.

Customer-Back Innovation: how we innovate

ITW innovates from the customer back, not from the research centre out. Divisions work with their key customers on hard technical problems and on improving business performance. Around 21,800 patents granted and pending through 2025 reflect the creativity of ITW’s people.

Decentralized, Entrepreneurial Culture: how we execute

ITW’s culture lets people think and act like owners. Everyone knows what is expected on the business model, strategy and values, and within that framework business teams make their own decisions and tailor the model to their customers and end markets. It is a culture of flexibility within the framework, with accountability and delivery.
