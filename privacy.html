Passive information collection

Some of the information we collect depends on the settings on your web browser or mobile device. Please check your browser or device if you want to learn what information it sends or how to change your settings. In addition to the information sent by your browser or device, as you navigate through a website or mobile app, certain information can be passively collected (that is, gathered without you actively providing it) using various technologies, such as navigational data collection.

Like most website operators, we and our approved partners use cookies and similar technologies, local shared objects (or any other successor tracker technology), Software Development Kits, and pixel tags that automatically receive and track certain data about how you and other visitors interact with our Website and Online Services, preferences expressed, and settings chosen. For example, we may collect information about the technology you use (such as browser, type of computer, operating system, mobile device and network used, and Internet service provider) and server log data (such as access dates and times, online features or pages viewed, system activity, and the third-party site or service you were using before interacting with our Website).

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device by websites, mobile apps, online media, and advertisements. Cookies served by the entity that operates the website you are visiting are “first party cookies”; cookies served by other companies (for example, a website analytics provider) are “third party cookies.” “Session cookies” last only as long as your browser or app is open. “Persistent cookies” survive after your browser or app is closed.

Local shared objects (also called Flash cookies or HTML5 cookies) and any successor technology are cookie-like data stored on a browser or computer by websites, ads, or third parties. Pixel tags (also called beacons or pixels) are small blocks of code on a web page, app, or advertisement that can retrieve certain information about your device and browser, such as device type, operating system, browser type and version, website visited, time of visit, referring website, and IP address. Software Development Kits (SDKs) function like pixels and cookies but operate in the mobile app context, allowing partners to collect certain information about user interaction with the app, the user device, and the network.

These and similar technologies are collectively referred to as “cookies and technologies.” We use them for analytics and research: to understand, improve, and research our Website and Online Services, and to better understand the interests of the people who interact with them. For example, we and our approved partners may use them to understand how you use Website features and to segment audiences for feature testing.

For example, we may obtain information about you from commercially available sources such as data aggregators and public databases (for example, job applicant information received from LinkedIn in connection with our hiring decisions). 5. What Are Your Rights and How You Can Exercise Them

The Data Protection Laws of India provide you the following rights about your personal information we collect, use, or disclose, subject to exemptions or exceptions prescribed by applicable laws from time to time:

  • Right to Notice. Before or at the time we collect personal information from you, you have the right to receive notice of the personal information to be collected and the purposes for which we use it. You also have the right to request that we disclose the categories of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources from which it was collected, the purpose for collecting or selling it, and the categories of third parties with whom we shared it.
  • Right of Access. You have the right to request (a) a summary of the personal information being processed by us and the processing activities undertaken with respect to it, and (b) the identities of all other entities or persons with whom we have shared your personal information, along with a description of the information shared.
  • Right to correction and erasure. You have the right to correction, completion, updating and erasure of your personal information for the processing of which you have previously given consent, including processing under the legitimate uses under any law for the time being in force. However, in certain situations we are not required to delete your personal information, such as when it is necessary to complete the transaction for which it was collected, to provide a good or service requested by you, to comply with a legal obligation, to engage in research, to secure our Website and Online Services, or to otherwise use it internally in a lawful manner compatible with the context in which you provided it, or for any other legitimate uses under applicable laws.
  • Right of grievance redressal. You have the right to raise any grievance relating to any act or omission by us regarding your personal information or your available rights. You also have the right to approach the Data Protection Board of India if you believe we have not redressed your grievance. You must first exhaust the grievance redressal opportunity with us before approaching the Board.
  • Right to nominate. You have the right to nominate any other individual who shall, in the event of your death or incapacity, exercise your rights in accordance with the applicable laws of India.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at infoindia@itw.com.

We value the security and confidentiality of your personal information. Therefore, if you exercise your right to notice, access, or deletion, we must first verify your identity to make sure you are the person about whom we have collected personal information. We verify every request carefully and ask that you provide a scanned copy of a government ID along with your request.
