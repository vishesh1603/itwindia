About ITW

Discover ITW

ITW is a global multi-industry manufacturer, founded in 1912 and headquartered in Glenview, Illinois. It is built around a differentiated, proprietary business model that its seven industry-leading segments use to deliver solid growth, strong margins and high returns.

ITW India Private Limited has operated in India for over three decades, establishing advanced manufacturing facilities, technical development centers, and sales offices across key industrial corridors including Gurgaon, Pune, Chennai, and Hyderabad. Through disciplined adherence to the ITW Business Model and 80/20 principles, ITW India delivers world-class engineering excellence, precision components, and sustainable industrial solutions tailored to Indian manufacturing and global export markets.
