Innovation

For more than 100 years, ITW has been turning ideas into innovations.

At ITW, innovation starts with customers. Customer-Back Innovation, a key part of the ITW Business Model, begins by understanding customers’ pain points and working out their root causes, so that workable solutions can be developed and tested.

ITW is also committed to environmentally responsible products that give cleaner, more efficient end use and proven performance, and it works with customers on clean-tech products for some of the world’s biggest challenges.

ITW Innovation Is Everywhere

You are never far from an ITW innovation, whether it secures components, preserves food or powers motion. Each solution is designed from the customer back to solve a real problem quietly, reliably and precisely.

Revolutionizing Construction Sites by Focusing on Our Customers

More than 25 years ago ITW’s Paslode division invented the first cordless nailer, giving contractors, builders and remodelers a new level of productivity and convenience.

Greener Technology for the Food Industry

Automation has transformed food service for over a century. Today customers also want greater sustainability and energy efficiency.

Leverage Diversity to Drive Innovation

Companies are building diversity and inclusion into leadership teams and boards, and into STEM education that attracts girls and young women.
