Construction Products

Premium engineered fastening for residential and commercial building.

The Construction Products segment supplies solutions for the places where people live and work. It serves residential, commercial and renovation markets with products for wood, engineered lumber, concrete and steel.

Its well-known brands lead where customers need higher productivity, better quality and strong safety standards, including offsite construction.

  • Innovative fastening solutions
  • Complementary fasteners and accessories
  • Construction design and engineering software
